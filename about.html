About Manhwa Mihon

What is Manhwa Mihon?

Manhwa Mihon Stories is a small online library of 30 popular manhwa, manga and web-novel style series. Each book has a cover, an author, a genre, a synopsis and a short overview page, in the spirit of reading platforms like Wattpad.

Every synopsis and overview is written in our own words for this project; the full series belong to their original creators. Book covers and illustrations are original vector artwork stored in the images folder, not the official cover art.

How to use it

Open the Library, choose a genre, and press Read Story on any cover. Use Details for a quick preview, and the heart to like a story.

Features

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript only.

Image slideshow

Search and filter

Find stories by title, author or genre in the Library.

ℹ️

Preview a book's synopsis from any card without leaving the page.

🔠

📱

Responsive

Designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
